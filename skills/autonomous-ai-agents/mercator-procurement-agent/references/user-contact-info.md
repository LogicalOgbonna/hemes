## When to share

Share contact info ONLY when:
1. The seller explicitly asks for it (phone number, email, "wie kann ich Sie erreichen")
2. You're at the closing stage (agreed on price, arranging pickup)
3. You've already sent our acceptance message and the seller asked to call/WhatsApp

## When NOT to share

- In initial contact messages — sellers don't need your number yet
- Before price negotiation is complete — gives away leverage
